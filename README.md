Analysed customer purchasing behaviour and loyalty program effectiveness across fast food, grocery, and petrol merchants using transaction data, survey insights, statistical testing, and customer segmentation. Identified 3 distinct customer segments (1. Cluster 1: At-Risk Low-Value Customers, 2. Cluster 2: Passive Contributors, 3. Cluster 3: High-Value Loyalists) and opportunities to improve retention, encourage cross-shopping, and enhance reward redemption. Developed data-driven recommendations, including targeted engagement strategies, cross-merchant incentives, and expanded redemption options to strengthen customer loyalty and support business growth.
